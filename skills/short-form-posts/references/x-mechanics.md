# X mechanics (researched 2026-08-30)

Strong heuristics, not physics. Engagement weights trace to X's open-sourced recommendation code; multipliers come from third-party analyses - use them to choose between options, never to promise a number.

## Threads

- Thread only when the idea has **steps or parts**. A single insight stretched to eight tweets is padding.
- Tweet 1 must stand alone and be worth a like on its own. It is the only one most people see.
- Every tweet earns the next: end on a turn, a number, or an unfinished thought.
- Numbering: `n/` beats `n/12` - a visible total tells people how much work they are signing up for.
- **Images carry the density.** A diagram in tweet 2 lets tweet 2 be one line instead of five.
- Last tweet: a question that is cheap to answer. "What am I missing?" outperforms "Let me know your thoughts" because it names the reply.

## What the algorithm rewards

| Signal | Weight vs a like |
| --- | --- |
| Like | 1x |
| Bookmark | ~12x |
| Repost | ~20x |
| Reply | ~27x |
| **You replying to a reply** | **~150x** |

The last row is the whole game and it is free. A post you cannot babysit is a post you should not send yet.

- **The first 30 minutes set the ceiling.** Early engagement velocity decides expansion or burial. Post only when the owner can spend the next 20-30 minutes replying; posting and walking away reportedly costs 30-60% of final reach.
- **Links are punished.** Severely for non-Premium accounts. Never a link in the opening post; put it in a reply, once, when it earns its place.
- **Hashtags are dead weight.** Content understanding is semantic; three or more can trip spam heuristics. Use zero.
- **Format preferences**, strongest first: native video (under ~2m20s), then native images, then text. An image is not decoration, it is distribution.
- **Tone is scored.** Constructive and specific travels further than combative.

## The posting ritual

1. Post when there are 30 free minutes, not when the draft is finished.
2. Reply to every reply with something substantive. This is the 150x.
3. Ask a question in the last tweet so there is something to reply to.
4. Link, if any, goes in a reply after the conversation has started.
5. Come back at 24h and record what actually happened.
